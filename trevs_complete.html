About Trev's Cafe and Restaurant

Our Story

Tucked away in Challis Street in Dickson, Trev's is a licensed Canberra restaurant and cafe where you can grab a tasty bite and enjoy good times with friends and family.

By day, Trev's offers a bustling and cosy atmosphere with great coffee and enticing breakfast and lunch options. Start your day with the appetizing Bircher muesli, lush scrambled eggs, and excellent coffee. For lunch, why not try one of Trev's famous pizzas or choose from the terrific specials board options.

As the sun goes down, the venue transforms into a casual restaurant, offering a variety of contemporary Australian cuisine of consistent quality and flair that is simply delicious.
