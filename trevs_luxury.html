About Trev's

Our Story

Established in the heart of Dickson, Trev's stands as a beacon of culinary excellence. We celebrate the art of hospitality through carefully sourced ingredients, expert preparation, and genuine warmth.

By day, our cafe buzzes with energy—from espresso connoisseurs to business professionals. By evening, we transform into an intimate dining destination where exceptional cuisine takes center stage.

Each dish reflects our commitment to quality, creativity, and respect for the craft of cooking.

Our Philosophy

Local Sourcing

We partner with regional producers to celebrate Canberra's finest produce and establish meaningful community connections.

Culinary Craft

Our kitchen applies traditional techniques with modern innovation, creating dishes of consistent excellence and flair.

Coffee Culture

From selection to brewing, every cup represents our dedication to the craft and art of exceptional coffee.

Beverage Curation

Our carefully assembled wine and beer selection complements our cuisine and elevates every dining experience.

Hospitality

We create memorable moments through attentive service and genuine care for every guest who walks through our doors.

Community

Trev's is more than a restaurant—it's a gathering place where people celebrate milestones and create lasting memories.
